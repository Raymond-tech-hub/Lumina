# Contributing to Lumina

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Lumina! Your feedback is essential for improving our AI-powered adaptive learning platform. Here are some ways you can contribute:

## Feedback Loops

1. **User Experience Feedback**: Share your thoughts on navigation, usability, and content.
2. **Feature Requests**: Suggest new features or improvements based on your experience.
3. **Bug Reports**: Help us identify issues by reporting bugs you come across.
